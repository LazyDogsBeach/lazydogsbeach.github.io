Domino Night (beta) terms of use

Last updated October 15, 2026

Domino Night (beta) covers the test versions of our domino party game. These terms are for its Roku channel only. It's run by John Nelson D'Albis, personally ("we" on this page). In short: it's a free test for people we invite, it may break, change or stop, please play fair, and Massachusetts law applies.

What these terms cover

These terms cover the Domino Night (beta) channel on Roku during the beta, including the phone game page (play.lazydogsbeach.com) and our server when they're used for a game hosted on that channel. They apply to testers and to everyone who plays in a game on the channel. They don't cover games hosted on any other version.

The beta

This is a test version for people we invite. It may have bugs, and it may change. It ends no later than February 12, 2027, when Roku removes this beta channel.

The beta is free. There's nothing to buy, and there are no ads.

Online play can change or stop

We may change the online service, limit how much or how often it can be used, pause it or stop it. An automatic limit on our running costs can end games, even in the middle of a game.

If we plan to stop online play before the beta ends, we'll tell testers at least 30 days ahead. A planned pause longer than a few days counts as stopping. We'll skip that notice only if the cost limit is reached, the service is being abused, or there's a security problem.

Who can play

Testers are adults (18 or older) in the US who we invite. Please don't share your access code. Other people can join a game from their phones, and a phone player under 18 needs a parent's OK.

Playing fair

Play fair, and keep display names decent. If a name or someone's play isn't, the game's host can remove that player before the game starts, or end the game at any time, and we may ask that player to stop playing. Don't overload, probe or reverse-engineer our server, and don't use the game for anything illegal.

Feedback

Sending feedback or ideas is up to you. If you send ideas, we may use them freely, and we don't owe you payment or credit for them. Please don't send art, music, code or written rules.

The app

The app is ours. You may use it for your own personal, non-commercial play during the beta.

Nothing here limits rights you have that the law doesn't let us limit.
