Domino Night (beta)

Domino Night (beta) is the test version of a dominoes game on Roku, and on Apple TV, iPad and Mac through TestFlight. It plays Mexican Train dominoes. The board is on the TV (or the iPad or Mac), and other players can't see your tiles on your phone.

Domino Night (beta) is run by John Nelson D'Albis.

How to play on Roku

Open Domino Night (beta) on your Roku. The TV shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone's camera. The first phone to scan hosts the game, and that person plays too. Use an iPhone with Safari for this.

Everyone else scans the code on the TV to join from their phone's web browser. There's nothing to install on the phones. You need 2 to 8 players, and the TV and each phone need the internet.

Who it's for

Testers are adults in the US, invited by John. They got a code and the steps to add it to their Roku. There's no public sign-up.

It's free. There's no sign-in in the game, and there are no purchases or ads.

It's unfinished, so things may break. The Roku test ends by February 12, 2027.
